Be Safe, Be Aware!

Try to make sure every link you click is trustworthy, you could've been hacked!

What went wrong?

The website you were just on was actually a bait page for hackers to steal login information.
Imagine the horror of a random individual having access to what should be personal or private info.

How can I prevent this?

The best place to start is always with a websites' domain and url. Simply put, is the site your opening up a .com, a .net or a .gov?
That part of a link lets you know where this site is coming from or being approved by so .com being from virtually anyone or .gov being from the government as a few examples.
Did you notice the strange parts of the link itself like how google was mispelled? Hackers are still people too at the end of the day and people can still make mistakes!

Stay Safe Stay Aware
