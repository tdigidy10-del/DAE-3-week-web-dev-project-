What Went Wrong?

The website you were just on was a bait page designed to steal your login credentials. Imagine a stranger having access to your most private accounts and personal data.

How Can I Prevent This?

  • ➤ Always check the domain — .com, .gov, .net tell you who's behind a site.
  • ➤ Look for the padlock icon and https:// in the address bar.
  • ➤ Notice small mistakes: outdated logos, odd fonts, or broken layouts.
  • ➤ Hackers are human — they make mistakes. Spot them before they spot you.
